About

Hi! I’m Jamie Simon. I got my PhD from UC Berkeley under Mike DeWeese, and I now run my own lab, named the Feature Lab (FLAB), dedicated to fundamental understanding of AI. We’re housed in the Redwood Center. My own position’s something in between a postdoc and a PI: in practice, I get to run a lab with none of the overhead required of proper professors, which works great for me. I’m also a research fellow at Imbue, who graciously fund my lab.

I believe in a well-rounded life. Apart from science, I play music, make art, go into nature, and have plenty of other adventures which you might learn about if you poke around this site.
