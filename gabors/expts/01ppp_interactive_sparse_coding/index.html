Projection pursuit by gradient ascent (tab 01j of page 01) on 16 × 16 CIFAR-10 patches, live on your GPU. Each unit climbs \(\mathbb E[G(w\cdot z)]\) on the unit sphere, orthogonal to the units already found, by plain gradient steps; type any \(G\) you like. Click a finished unit to see where its objective comes from. Advanced settings has a raw switch (no whitening, variances kept).

how it works, in one paragraph

Patches are centred, projected onto their top \(M\) principal components and whitened there, so \(z\) has identity covariance and every projection \(u = w\cdot z\) with \(|w| = 1\) has unit variance: a unit Gaussian is the null. Unit \(k\) starts from a random direction orthogonal to the \(k-1\) found; each step takes the gradient of \(J(w) = \mathbb E[G(w\cdot z)]\) over all \(N\) patches, removes its components along \(w\) and along the found units, moves by the learning rate times what is left, Gram-Schmidts against the found units and renormalises. It stops when the remaining gradient is below the tolerance times the whole gradient, or at the step budget, and is then frozen. On the GPU a step is three small kernels over the \(M \times N\) whitened data; nothing is read back except a convergence number every ten steps, so a 64-unit run at \(N\) = 50,000 takes seconds. The pictures are the units mapped back to pixels.

advanced settings
16 × 16 patches sampled from 10,000 CIFAR-10 images; the whole set is used for every gradient the 256 pixels are projected onto their top M principal components and whitened there; M is the most units there can be how many directions to find, one after another, each orthogonal to the ones before step along the gradient on the sphere; 0.3-1 converges in a few hundred steps for log cosh a unit that has not converged by then is frozen as it is a unit has converged when the part of the gradient that can still move it (along the sphere, orthogonal to the found units) is below this fraction of the whole gradient whitening divides each principal component by its standard deviation; eigenvalues below ε × the largest are raised to that floor so near-empty directions are not blown up. At 10-4 it never binds on CIFAR patches at M ≤ 192; 10-2 acts as a low-pass filter unchecked: whitened, every principal component scaled to unit variance, so a unit Gaussian is the null for every direction. Checked: the variances are kept (one common scale, so a random direction has unit variance on average); the plots then use u/σ so the Gaussian comparison is variance-matched, and σ is reported
edit the list of objectives
One expression per line, in u; every one is maximised, so write -log(cosh(u)) for the log cosh contrast. Functions: sin cos tan exp log cosh sinh tanh abs sqrt sign relu softplus max min; ** or ^; pi, e. Square brackets are indicators: [abs(u) > 3] is 1 where true, 0 elsewhere (its derivative is a Gaussian bump of width 0.1 at the edge, so it still pushes). The list is kept in this browser.
