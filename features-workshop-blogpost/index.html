What is a feature? Perspectives, cruxes, and paths forwards

Introduction

An enormous amount of scientific effort these last years has hinged on the following questions: what are neural network features? How should we operationalize that notion and measure them? To what degree do we need mathematical principle behind such a notion vs. just getting it to work empirically?

We held a workshop in Berkeley on October 20-22 of this year to discuss these questions. We brought together experts from different areas of the field, including mechanistic interpretability, singular learning theory, and learning mechanics, to discuss, identify cruxes, and seek a path forwards.

What follows are the results of these discussions. These results reveal a diverse and divided field with differing (but often complementary) perspectives on the nature of features and the path forwards.
