About

The Redwood Center for the Science of Intelligence is a research institute at UC Berkeley that seeks the fundamental scientific principles of natural and artificial intelligence. We're housed in Warren Hall within the Helen Wills Neuroscience Institute.

Get involved

Join us for a seminar or a journal club meeting, or reach out to one of our members directly. Prospective PhD students apply through one of Berkeley's graduate programs:

  • Helen Wills Neuroscience Institute
  • Vision Science
  • Physics
  • EECS
  • Bioengineering
  • Biophysics
  • Statistics
  • Mathematics
