About

The Redwood Center is a group of students, postdocs and faculty at UC Berkeley building mathematical and computational models of perception, cognition, learning and motor function. We're one of several research centers within the Helen Wills Neuroscience Institute.

About the Center

We develop mathematical and computational models of the neurobiological mechanisms behind perception, cognition, learning and motor function. We train students at UC Berkeley in these ideas and methods, and we collaborate with experimental neuroscientists to test our theories.

Our members come from neuroscience, vision science, psychology, physics, electrical engineering, computer science and mathematics, and we bring ideas from these fields together to develop and test theories of neural computation.

We host seminars, courses and journal clubs throughout the year, and previously hosted the CRCNS summer courses on mining and modeling neuroscience data. The Center grew out of the Redwood Neuroscience Institute.

The Redwood Center values diversity of opinion, background and culture, and encourages participation from members of underrepresented groups, including women, people of color and LGBTQ+ people. Consistent with UC Berkeley policy, we do not tolerate sexual or racial discrimination or harassment.
