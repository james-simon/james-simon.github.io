Postdoctoral positions

The Redwood Center invites applications for postdoctoral positions in the science of intelligence. Postdocs join one or more of our five research pillars and work across the Center's labs, with the freedom to set their own research direction.

Research areas

  1. Learning mechanics
  2. Structure of data and learned representations
  3. Principles of mechanistic interpretability and neural computation
  4. Dynamics of computation
  5. Collective intelligence

Requirements

  • A PhD in physics, computer science, neuroscience, mathematics, statistics or a related field, completed by the start date.
  • A strong quantitative background and a record of independent research.
  • Interest in at least one of the research areas above, and in working across them.
  • Experience with theory, large-scale experiments, or both.

Terms

  • Two-year appointment, renewable for a third year.
  • Flexible start date.
  • Salary and benefits follow the University of California postdoctoral scale.

How to apply

Email a single PDF to [address to be announced] containing your CV, a research statement of up to two pages naming the area or areas you'd work in, and the names of three references. Review begins [date to be announced] and continues until positions are filled.

Prospective PhD students

The Redwood Center doesn't admit graduate students directly. Our faculty advise students through UC Berkeley's PhD programs, including Neuroscience, Physics, Vision Science, and Electrical Engineering and Computer Sciences. To work with us, apply to one of these programs and name the Redwood faculty you'd like to work with in your statement.

See our faculty and their labs, and read about our research.
