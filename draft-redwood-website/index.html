Redwood Center for the Science of Intelligence

The Redwood Center is an interdepartmental research institute which seeks the scientific principles behind natural and artificial intelligence.

Seeking a science of intelligence

We aim to develop a first-principles, quantitative science of how intelligence is learned, represented, interpreted, computed, and organized in brains and machines.

Our research has five pillars:

  1. 1

    Learning mechanics

    How do neural networks learn?

  2. 2

    Data and representations

    How does data shape learning?

  3. 3

    Interpretability and neural computation

    How do biological and artificial networks compute?

  4. 4

    Dynamics of computation

    How does intelligence unfold in time?

  5. 5

    Collective intelligence

    What laws emerge when agents interact?

Read about our research and explore our papers.
